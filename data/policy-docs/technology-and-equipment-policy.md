# Technology and Equipment Policy

## Personal electronics

**TECH-01 — Personal electronics are not reimbursable.** This includes, and is not limited to:
headphones and earbuds, smart watches and fitness trackers, tablets and e-readers, games
consoles, cameras and drones, televisions, portable speakers, and item trackers. Accessories made
for such a device — cases, chargers, screen protectors — count as personal electronics too.
Purchase of any of these on an expense report is a **high** exception, regardless of stated
business purpose.

## Accessories and peripherals

**TECH-02 — Peripherals under $200.** Computer accessories and peripherals — cables, adapters,
docking stations, mice, keyboards, monitors, laptop stands, webcams — are reimbursable up to
**$200 per item**, judged on the item's unit price in USD. Any accessory or peripheral above
$200 requires a pre-approval reference (see EXC-02); without one it is a **medium** exception.

## Phones and software

**TECH-03 — Mobile handsets.** Mobile phone hardware is procured centrally by IT and is never
reimbursable on an expense report. This is a **high** exception.

**TECH-04 — Software subscriptions.** Any software or SaaS subscription on an expense report
requires a manager approval reference (see EXC-02). Without one this is a **low** exception.
