# Meals and Entertainment Policy

Applies to all employees of Northwind Global unless a regional addendum overrides a rule
(see `regional-addendum.md`).

## Meal limits

**MEAL-01 — Daily meal cap.** Meal spend is capped at **$75 per person per day** for domestic
travel and **$100 per person per day** for international travel. Spend above the cap is a
**medium** exception. For this rule:

- a **meal receipt** is one declared as Meals - Individual, Meals - Team or Client Entertainment
  that contains food or drink — or, if it is not itemized, any receipt declared as one of those types;
- **meal spend** is the USD total of the receipt's food, drink and gratuity lines (categories
  `food_beverage`, `alcohol` and `service_fee`) — anything else on the bill, such as a gift, is
  left out. For a receipt that is not itemized, it is the full receipt total;
- **per person** means divided by the attendee count, or by one when no attendee count is recorded;
- **per day** means the per-person amounts of all of one employee's meal receipts on the same
  calendar date, added together;
- travel is **international** when the receipt's currency differs from the currency of the
  employee's home country, and **domestic** otherwise.

MEAL-01 applies to every meal receipt, whether or not it is part of a trip: "domestic" and
"international" only choose which cap applies, and on a day that mixes the two, the higher cap
applies. When a day's total is over the cap, raise MEAL-01
on every meal receipt that contributes to that day. For APAC and EMEA employees the regional cap in
`regional-addendum.md` replaces both figures, and a breach is reported under REG-02 or REG-03
instead of MEAL-01.

**MEAL-02 — Attendee documentation.** Any meal receipt (as defined in MEAL-01) whose receipt total
is above **$150** in USD must record the number of attendees. A missing attendee count is a **low** exception.

## Alcohol

**ALC-01 — Alcohol requires a documented entertainment context.** Alcohol is reimbursable
**only** on a receipt declared as Client Entertainment that records an attendee count of two
or more. Alcohol on
any other receipt — an individual or team meal, or any receipt without an attendee count — is a
**high** exception.

**ALC-02 — Alcohol per-attendee cap.** Where alcohol is permitted under ALC-01, it is capped at
**$30 per attendee**: the USD total of the alcohol lines, divided by the attendee count. Spend
above that cap is a **medium** exception.

*Scope of ALC-01 and ALC-02.* These two rules govern alcohol served on the premises — at restaurants,
bars and hotels (MCC 5812, 5813 and 7011). Alcohol bought from any other merchant is retail and is
governed by ALC-03 alone.

**ALC-03 — Retail alcohol.** Alcohol purchased from a package store, liquor store, or any other
merchant that is not a restaurant, bar or hotel is **never** reimbursable, regardless of business context. This is a
**high** exception. See also MCC-01 in `merchant-category-risk.md`.

**Note on identification.** Alcohol is determined by the *nature of the item purchased*, not by
the words in its name. Food prepared with alcohol (for example beer-battered fish, wine sauce,
rum cake), non-alcoholic cocktails (for example a virgin mojito), and non-alcoholic mixed drinks
(for example an Arnold Palmer) are **food and beverage**, not alcohol. Barware and drinkware are
household goods (see `non-reimbursable-items.md`).

## Entertainment

**ENT-01 — Entertainment venues.** Golf, sporting events, theatre, concerts, and similar
venue spend requires prior VP approval, recorded as an approval reference (see EXC-02). Without
one this is a **medium** exception.

**ENT-02 — Gambling.** Gambling spend of any kind is prohibited and is a **high** exception.
