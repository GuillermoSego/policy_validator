# Non-Reimbursable Items

## Personal care

**PERS-01 — Personal care and grooming.** Toiletries, cosmetics, razors, haircuts, salon
services, spa treatments, and massage are not reimbursable. This is a **medium** exception.

*Baggage-delay exception.* Where checked baggage is delayed more than 24 hours, essential
toiletries are reimbursable up to **$150**, provided the delay reference is recorded (see EXC-02).

**PERS-02 — Clothing and footwear.** Clothing, footwear, and fashion accessories such as belts,
ties and bags are not reimbursable;
a **medium** exception. The same $150 baggage-delay exception applies: one $150 allowance per
delay, shared between toiletries and clothing. Above it, the exception cites every line involved.

**PERS-03 — Tobacco and vaping.** Cigarettes, cigars, loose tobacco, vaping devices and
e-liquids are not reimbursable. This is a **high** exception.

## Household

**HOUSE-01 — Household goods.** Cleaning supplies, laundry products, household paper goods
(paper towels, toilet paper, tissues), kitchenware and drinkware, bedding, furniture, small
appliances, and home décor are not reimbursable, whether bought for home or for an office — office
stock of this kind is bought centrally. This is a **medium** exception.

## Health and services

**HEALTH-01 — Over-the-counter medication.** OTC medication is reimbursable only on a receipt
linked to a trip, up to **$25** in total across all of that trip's receipts. Above $25, or on a
receipt with no trip record, it is a **low** exception, raised on each such receipt and citing its
medication lines. Prescription
medication is never reimbursable on an expense report and is a **medium** exception.

**LAUNDRY-01 — Laundry and dry cleaning.** Reimbursable only on trips of **four or more
nights**, using the trip length on the receipt. On shorter trips, or on a receipt with no trip
record, it is a **low** exception.
